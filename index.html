Mingchao Xu 许铭潮

Applied AI Researcher & Engineer 应用 AI 算法研究员与工程师

I am an applied AI researcher and engineer at China Telecom in Suzhou. My work focuses on multimodal large language models and computer vision, with an emphasis on developing reliable systems for real-world applications. 我目前在中国电信苏州公司从事应用 AI 研发，主要关注多模态大模型与计算机视觉，致力于将算法研究转化为可在真实业务环境中可靠运行的系统。

Previously, I worked on end-to-end autonomous-driving perception at Momenta, parking and BEV perception at HoloMatic, and map-change discovery at Alibaba Amap. I received my M.Eng. from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and my B.Eng. from Harbin Institute of Technology. 此前，我曾在 Momenta 从事端到端自动驾驶感知，在禾多科技从事泊车与 BEV 感知，并在阿里高德研发地图变化发现算法。我硕士毕业于中国科学院自动化研究所，本科毕业于哈尔滨工业大学。

Research interests:研究方向： Multimodal LLMs Computer Vision Autonomous Driving 2D / 3D Perception

Failsafe perception under extreme conditions 极端场景下的 Failsafe 视觉感知
